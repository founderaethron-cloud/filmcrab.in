SAMPLE SCRIPT - "KAYAL KARA" (test fixture, original text)

1. EXT. ALAPPUZHA BACKWATERS - DAWN

Mist over the water. A small canoe drifts. SAJAN (30s, fisherman) rows slowly.

SAJAN
ഇന്നും മീൻ ഒന്നുമില്ല. Enthu cheyyum ini?

2. INT. SAJAN'S HOUSE, KITCHEN - MORNING

AMMINI (60s) sits by the stove, stirring a pot.

AMMINI
നീ ഇന്നും വെറും കയ്യോടെ വന്നോ? Ente daivame.

SAJAN
Amme, kaalam mosham aanu. Pinne, kadam theerkkanam.

AMMINI
കടം! എല്ലാം കടം. നിന്റെ അച്ഛനും ഇങ്ങനെ തന്നെ ആയിരുന്നു.

Sajan looks away. A letter lies on the table.

3. EXT. ALAPPUZHA JETTY - DAY

Tourists crowd the jetty. Houseboats lined up. MATHEW (50s, boat owner, sharp shirt) argues with a tout.

MATHEW
You can't park here. This is my slot.

TOUT
Chetta, ellavarkum vendi aanu ee jetty.

Sajan watches from a distance.

4. INT. MATHEW'S HOUSEBOAT - DAY

Sajan enters, cap in hand.

SAJAN
Mathew chetta, oru joli tharumo?

MATHEW
Joli? Nee ente boat odikkumo?

SAJAN
Odikkam. Ethu storm-ilum.

MATHEW
(smiling)
Storm varatte. Appo kaanam.

5. EXT. KAYAL - NIGHT - RAIN

A storm breaks. Sajan's canoe is tossed. He spots a drifting boat with a woman, RENU (20s), waving.

RENU
Help! Please!

SAJAN
പിടിച്ചോ! കയ്യിൽ പിടിച്ചോ!

FIGHT: Sajan leaps into the water. Rain, waves, a rope. He drags her to the canoe.

6. INT. HOSPITAL CORRIDOR - NIGHT

Sajan sits, drenched. A DOCTOR approaches.

DOCTOR
She'll be fine. Who is she to you?

SAJAN
Ariyilla. Kayalil ninnu kittiyatha.

7. EXT. HOSPITAL COMPOUND - PUNCHA - DAY

Renu, wearing borrowed clothes, finds Sajan by a tea stall.

RENU: നന്ദി. നിങ്ങൾ എന്റെ ജീവൻ രക്ഷിച്ചു.
SAJAN: അയ്യോ, അത് ഒന്നുമില്ല.
RENU: എനിക്ക് ഒരു കാര്യം പറയാനുണ്ട്. Mathew sir-nte boat il ninnu aanu njan veezhunnathu.

Sajan freezes.

SONG: "KAYAL PAATTU" - Sajan and Renu walk by the backwaters. Montage. Sunrise to sunset.

8. INT. MATHEW'S OFFICE - NIGHT

Mathew counts cash. Phone rings. He listens, face hardens.

MATHEW
She is alive? Ayal ariyaruthu.

CUT TO BLACK.
